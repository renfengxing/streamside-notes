Outdoor notes session

A simple planning sheet for a short outdoor writing session. Date: __________ Location: __________

1. Prepare

  • □ Choose a familiar place where writing is permitted.
  • □ Pack your notebook and pencil.
  • □ Write down one thing you want to observe.

2. Observe and write

  • □ Find a suitable place to pause.
  • □ Note three colors or shapes you notice.
  • □ Sketch one detail and label it.

3. Review

  • □ Add a date and a descriptive title.
  • □ Check that your notes make sense to you.
  • □ Take your materials with you when leaving.
